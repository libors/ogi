Jobs and battles per format

APS and the battles it took

Each point is a comparison that measured the rumble's APS: its opponents were the whole rumble, or a sample of it whose groups weigh as many bots as they stand for in the rumble. Subsets (the strong bots, some groups, bots picked by a trait) give how we do against them, not where we stand, so they are left out; the table under each chart says which sets counted. The horizontal axis counts every battle the workers played in the format by the time the job ended, subsets and recordings included: that is what the APS cost. APS is the harness's local estimate on the worker, not a rumble result. Click a point to open its job.
